# **CA/Browser Forum IPR Policy Agreement**

This CA/Browser Forum Intellectual Property Rights Policy Agreement (the “Agreement”) constitutes a binding agreement by and among all individuals and legal entities that execute this Agreement (each, a “Participant,” and collectively, the “Participants”).

Each Participant enters into this Agreement in consideration of the mutual promises and commitments of the other Participants.

## **1\. Incorporation of IPR Policy**

Each Participant agrees to comply with and be bound by the CA/Browser Forum Intellectual Property Rights Policy, version 1.4 (the “IPR Policy”), as adopted and amended from time to time by the CA/Browser Forum. The IPR Policy is incorporated herein by reference.

Each Participant acknowledges that certain obligations under the IPR Policy may survive termination of participation in a Working Group or withdrawal from the Forum, as more fully described in the IPR Policy.

## **2\. Scope of Commitment**

By executing this Agreement, each Participant agrees that, with respect to each CA/Browser Forum Working Group that the Participant joins, all contributions to such Working Group and all participation in such Working Group activities shall be subject to the IPR Policy.

The obligations undertaken by each Participant under this Agreement are made to each and every other Participant. Each Participant acknowledges that each other Participant is an intended beneficiary of this Agreement.

## **3\. Entities and Individuals**

If a Participant is a legal entity, this Agreement binds the entity identified below.

If a Participant is an individual signing in a personal capacity, this Agreement binds only that individual.

An individual signing on behalf of a legal entity represents and warrants that they are duly authorized to execute this Agreement on behalf of the identified entity and to bind that entity to its terms.

An individual signing in a personal capacity represents that they are not executing this Agreement on behalf of any legal entity and that, to the best of their knowledge, their participation in the Forum does not violate any contractual or legal obligations to which they are subject.

## **4\. Affiliates**

If a Participant is a legal entity, the Participant agrees that this Agreement and the incorporated IPR Policy shall apply to the Participant and to any Affiliate that the Participant has the legal authority to bind with respect to intellectual property rights defined in the IPR Policy.

For purposes of this Agreement, “Affiliate” means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with the Participant.

To the extent a Participant does not have legal authority to bind a particular Affiliate, such Affiliate shall not be bound unless it separately executes this Agreement.

## **5\. Condition of Participation**

Execution and continued effectiveness of this Agreement are conditions of participation in the CA/Browser Forum and its Working Groups. Failure to execute or maintain this Agreement in effect may result in suspension or termination of participation rights in accordance with the Forum’s Bylaws and applicable ballots.

## **6\. Interpretation**

This Agreement shall be interpreted consistently with the IPR Policy. In the event of any inconsistency between this Agreement and the IPR Policy with respect to intellectual property matters, the IPR Policy shall control.
